Privacy Policy for MicroChef

Last Updated: February 2, 2026

MicroChef ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by MicroChef.

By accessing or using our application, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you use the app:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name (optional) and email address.
  • User Content: We store information about your pantry inventory, shopping lists, and recipe preferences to provide the core functionality of the app.
  • Technical Data: We may collect device information, such as your mobile device ID, model, manufacturer, and operating system version.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Service: To manage your account, track your pantry, and provide personalized recipe suggestions.
  • To Improve the Service: To understand how users interact with the app and to optimize the user experience.
  • Communication: To send you technical notices, updates, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Third-Party Services

MicroChef uses Supabase as its backend provider for authentication and data storage.

  • Your account and app data are stored on Supabase's secure servers.
  • Supabase may process your data according to their own Privacy Policy, which we encourage you to review at supabase.com/privacy.

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as we need it to provide the MicroChef service and to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. When you delete your account, we will delete your personal data from our active databases, subject to any legal requirements to retain certain information.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Correction: The right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Deletion: The right to request that we erase your personal data.
  • Portability: The right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
